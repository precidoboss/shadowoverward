OBJECTIVES

Full run: find the four VHS tapes. Tape 01 is in Ward A, Tape 02 in Ward B, Tape 03 in the wall safe in Dr. Holloway's office (code 1974), and Tape 04 on the autopsy table in the morgue. Restore generator power, stop Shadow 47, and escape through the fire exit.

Quick run: recover the rifle and the tape, open the bedroom safe (code 0 4 0 7, shown on the skelevision), take the high-voltage fuse to the generator, stop the creature, and walk into the portal.

SURVIVAL

  • Running is loud. Walk or crouch near Subject 407 to stay hidden.
  • Direct flashlight beams draw its attention. Turn the light off when it is close.
  • When your camcorder glitches and your heartbeat races, it is near. Hide.
  • Your health slowly recovers when you stay out of harm's way.
